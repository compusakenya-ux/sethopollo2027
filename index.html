Our 4-Point Pillar Plan

My Campaign Priorities

Practical, grassroots-first solutions designed for every household, youth, and trader across Jomvu Kuu Ward.

🛡️

Security

  • I will work for safer streets and better lighting in Jomvu, Aldina and Jitoni
  • I will strengthen community policing partnerships
🎓

Education

  • I will improve public school infrastructure: classrooms, desks, toilets and labs
  • I will back bursaries and support for learners in need
🏍️

Small Business

  • I will support the boda-boda industry: safe stages, licensing and SACCOs
  • I will stand with traders and make starting a business in Mombasa County seamless
  • I will push legislation to lower Single Business Permit (SBP) costs
💧

Clean Drinking Water

  • I will fight for reliable, safe and affordable water in every household
  • I will deliver new boreholes, pipelines and water points
